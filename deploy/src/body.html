XRLibraries Campus

A museum with a living-history Wax Hall, a library, a hands-on workshop school and a recess yard about XR in libraries, built to walk through in a web browser.

An unofficial concept made for XRLibraries. The museum and library use facts from public sources, mainly xrlibraries.com and the news reports cited on each card. Wax Hall figures are generic reenactors, not likenesses of real people. The staff are virtual and answer common questions on their own. The rides in the Recess Yard are made up for fun. The people are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT License, © 2020 Microsoft).
